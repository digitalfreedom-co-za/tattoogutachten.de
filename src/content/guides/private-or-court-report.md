## What is a private expert report?

A private expert report (Privatgutachten) is a report commissioned by one of the parties rather than by the court. It can be prepared before, during or entirely without court proceedings. Clients can be individuals, businesses such as tattoo and piercing studios, or their legal representatives.

A private report can help to place the facts in a professional context, remove uncertainty and provide a factual basis for an out-of-court settlement. Not every disagreement has to go to court straight away. More on the page [Private reports and expert opinions](/services/private-reports/).

## What is a court report?

A court report answers the questions the court has put to the expert in proceedings. When the court appoints the expert, the commission comes directly from the competent court. The basis is the documents available and, where necessary, the expert's own examinations. More on the page [Reports for courts](/services/court-reports/).

## How do the two differ?

| Feature | Private expert report | Court report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commissioned by | You, your studio or your lawyer | The competent court |
| Questions | Agreed in the expert services agreement | Questions put by the court |
| Timing | Before, during or without court proceedings | In pending proceedings |
| Fees | By time spent, agreed in writing beforehand | Under the JVEG, paid through the court |

## Can a private report be used in court?

A private report can be submitted in court proceedings and can help to present the facts from a professional point of view. It is not, however, automatically equivalent to a report obtained by the court. The weight it carries in the specific proceedings is decided by the competent court.

## Who pays for the report?

For a private report, the fee terms agreed in the expert services agreement apply. The expected costs are discussed before you commission the report and recorded in writing. When the court appoints the expert, remuneration follows the JVEG (Justizvergütungs- und Entschädigungsgesetz, the German Judicial Remuneration and Compensation Act) and is handled through the court. Details are under [Process and fees](/process-and-fees/#fees).

Whether legal expenses insurance covers the cost of an expert report can only be confirmed by your insurer. It is best to check this before commissioning.

## Which report do I need?

That depends on whether proceedings are already under way and what you want to achieve. Whether a private report makes legal sense in your case is something to discuss with your lawyer. Whether an assessment is possible from a professional point of view and what scope it would have, we clarify in the free first consultation. The same applies to both types of report: the assessment is independent and impartial and does not depend on who commissions it. Further answers about commissioning a report are in the [FAQ](/faq/#commissioning).
