## What is the difference between a defect and taste?

A defect is an error in the execution that can be established on craft grounds. Taste is your personal view of the result. A tattoo that feels disappointing and a tattoo with a demonstrable error in its execution are two very different things.

A tattoo can be cleanly executed and still fall short of your expectations. Equally, a design can look appealing at first glance and still show flaws in its workmanship.

## Which features does an expert examine?

An expert examination can cover, among other things:

- linework, contours and technical execution
- evenness of shading and colour areas
- distribution and uptake of ink in the skin
- how the design was put into practice and the quality of the craft
- irregularities, scarring and possible defects in execution
- differences between the agreed design and the actual result

The aim is to identify possible workmanship errors, document them and place them in context in a way others can follow. What a report covers in detail is shown on the page [Tattoo reports](/services/tattoo-reports/).

## What role does the agreed design play?

The design is an important benchmark. If the result departs clearly from what was agreed, that is one of the points the assessment examines. Keep the design or sketch, together with any messages in which the motif, size or placement were discussed.

## Why does this take practical experience?

Assessing the quality of a tattoo properly requires an understanding of how tattooing works in practice. Looking at the finished design is not enough. What matters is knowledge of working methods, of how the tattooing process interacts with the skin, and of the possible causes of irregularities in the work.

## Are photos enough for the assessment?

Often for a first view, not always for a final assessment. Not every feature of a tattoo can be judged reliably from pictures; in that case an examination in person is needed. Tips for useful pictures are in the guide [Photographing a tattoo for assessment](/guides/photographing-a-tattoo-for-assessment/).

## What follows from the result?

The report sets out expert findings. Whether a defect that has been found gives rise to legal claims is a legal question for a lawyer. Even where the report finds no defect, it brings clarity, for client and studio alike. Further answers are in the [FAQ](/faq/#basics).
