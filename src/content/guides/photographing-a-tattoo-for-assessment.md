## Why do photos matter so much?

Photos are often the first basis on which an expert judges whether an assessment makes sense. In the free first consultation we go through the photographs and documents you have.

Photos also record a condition. Because the passage of time and later changes can make the assessment harder, pictures from the session and the healing phase are often especially useful later on.

## How do I photograph my tattoo properly?

The aim is pictures that show lines, shading and colour areas as they really are. What works well:

- even daylight without hard shadows and without a flash that reflects off the skin
- one overview shot that shows the whole tattoo and the part of the body
- close-ups of the areas that concern you, in focus and taken straight on
- several pictures from slightly different angles
- no filters, no editing and no beautifying by the camera app
- keep the original files, not just screenshots or copies shrunk by a messaging app

Note the date of each series of photos if the file does not show it.

## Which other photos help?

Any pictures that show how things developed: photos straight after the session, from the healing phase and from the time afterwards. Add the agreed design or sketch, so that motif and result can be compared. With piercings, too, photos showing the position and the surrounding area help.

## Are photos enough for an expert report?

Not always. A first expert view may be possible on the basis of clear photographs. A final assessment can require an examination in person, because not every feature of a tattoo can be judged reliably from pictures alone. The features examined are listed on the page [Tattoo reports](/services/tattoo-reports/).

## Do the photos replace documentation by an expert?

No. Your own photos are a valuable addition. If you want to have the tattoo touched up, reworked or removed, its condition should be documented by an expert first. The page [Preserving evidence with a private report](/services/private-reports/#preserving-evidence) explains how a private expert report does this.

## What do I do with the photos?

Bring them to the free first consultation, together with the invoice, any agreement, the aftercare instructions and your correspondence so far. Which documents are needed in your case we clarify in the conversation. Further answers are in the [FAQ](/faq/#assessment).
